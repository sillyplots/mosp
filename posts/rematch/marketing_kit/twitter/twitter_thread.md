## Post 1 (attach `../assets/rematch_win_rates.png`)
Today the booth will say it's tough to beat a team twice.

We checked 1,299 NFL rematches. The team that won the first meeting wins 57.9% of the time. 🧵

## Post 2
Controlling for team quality and home field, the "rematch penalty" is zero. Slightly positive, in fact.

Being better at football in October remains a durable asset in December.

## Post 3
Where the myth comes from:
• Prior winners on the road: 51.8%
• Teams that won Game 1 on a turnover fluke: 45.2%

The booth has rediscovered home-field advantage and regression to the mean, and mistaken them for a curse.

## Post 4
Field test: Seattle beat Arizona 31-7 in Week 2. Rematch today.

Our model: Seattle 63%.
Should Arizona win, please attribute it to the 37% chance, not to the difficulty of beating a team twice.
