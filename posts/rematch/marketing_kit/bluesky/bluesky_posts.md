## Post 1 (attach `../assets/rematch_win_rates.png`)
The broadcast will tell you it's tough to beat a team twice.

Across 1,299 NFL rematches, the team that won the first meeting wins 57.9% of the time. After controlling for quality and home field, there is no rematch penalty.
