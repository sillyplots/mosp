# IDEA LEDGER — Ministry of Silly Plots

Lifecycle: `PROPOSED → RED-TEAMED → APPROVED | NIXED`. Approved ideas get a
row in [PIPELINE.md](PIPELINE.md). Nixed ideas stay here with cause of death —
the graveyard is a reference collection, not a landfill.

Every idea entry uses this format:

```
## <Codename> — <one-line premise>
- **Stage:** PROPOSED | RED-TEAMED | APPROVED | NIXED
- **Premise:** the ridiculous question, stated seriously
- **Data angle:** what dataset(s) would make this real analysis, and whether they're gettable
- **The plot:** what the money chart looks like if the hypothesis holds
- **Red-team file:** link to reports/<date>-red-team-<codename>.md once reviewed
```

An idea is not APPROVED until it has survived red team. No exceptions,
including for ideas the Minister is fond of.
